## First reading

The share of errors that belong to failure types not yet seen is estimated accurately everywhere. For planted types
the true share is 31.5%, 7.5% and 1.1% at 300, 1,000 and 3,000 rows, and the estimate (1 - sample coverage) is off by
+1.4, +0.5 and +0.3 percentage points on average. Across every model, dataset, flip rate and number of planted types
the mean error stays within 3.3 points, and for k-means error clusters within 0.6 points. The total number of types
remains hard: the bias-calibrated bootstrap removes most of the average bias (ACE: -13.5% to +8.9% at 300 rows, about
-5% and 0% at 1,000 and 3,000) but its intervals still cover the truth in only 63-90% of runs. These are observations
from an unregistered pilot, not findings.
